Common questions

Before you sign up

What am I actually paying for?

Configuration, workflow hosting/management as specified, testing and support for a business automation. You receive access to the deployed result, not ownership of third-party AI models or a downloadable app. Any handover or ownership terms are defined in the proposal.

How does the chatbot get onto my site?

Once you approve the setup, we give you the small website embed snippet or coordinate with whoever manages your site. The bot is tested before public launch.

Do customers need to install anything?

No. For website chat, customers use the chat widget on your existing site. Your staff view inquiries in the connected sheet, inbox or other agreed tool.

Can it answer my business phone?

Not with the listed Starter or Receptionist package. Voice calling requires a separate integration and quote.

Does it automatically confirm appointments?

No. The standard receptionist collects appointment requests and leaves scheduling confirmation to your team unless a separately scoped calendar integration is built and tested.

Can I cancel or take the system with me?

Cancellation, data export, account ownership and transition arrangements will be included in your written service agreement. Ask about these before ordering.
